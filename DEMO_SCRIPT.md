# COMMONS: 3-minute demo script

**Before recording:** Settings → Reset demo. Have a funded NEAR testnet wallet ready and `NEXT_PUBLIC_COMMONS_CONTRACT_ID` set.

| Time | Screen | Say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Landing hero | "Small businesses pay the solo price. COMMONS pools their orders." Let the hero animation run once: three stacks merge into one pallet and the per-carton price drops. |
| 0:15 | Scroll the story | Five steps play as you scroll: three New York businesses, four suppliers compared, the cheap offer rejected, the deal that fits, and the commitment locked on NEAR. |
| 0:35 | "Add your order" | Drag the slider. "This is the real Demand Compiler running in the browser. Add 800 cartons and the group crosses Empire Paper Mills' 5,000 minimum, so the price drops again." |
| 0:50 | Open app → **Bloom Bakery** | Dashboard: live pools across five cities, savings in NEAR with USD in brackets, one approval waiting on Maya. |
| 1:00 | Discover → cartons pool → **Run agent negotiation** | Stages light up. "Apex quotes $1.80, the lowest sticker price, but $900 delivery pushes Ortiz Coffee to $2.00 a carton, over its $1.90 limit. The Financial Guardian rejects it." |
| 1:25 | Proposal B | "The agent re-searches 28 combinations on landed cost. Hudson Box Co.: $1.89 landed for everyone, 13 of 13 checks passed, $3,130 saved versus buying alone." |
| 1:45 | Review and approve | Tick the acknowledgement (the button is disabled until you do) and approve. |
| 2:00 | Persona switcher | Switch to Ortiz Coffee Roasters, approve; switch to Loom & Thread, approve. "Nothing is approved automatically." |
| 2:15 | NEAR panel | Connect wallet → Create pool → Anchor terms hash → Sign 3 approvals → Record settlement. |
| 2:40 | Transactions | Off-chain approval records (no hash) next to verified testnet transactions; open one in NearBlocks. |
| 2:50 | Close | "Collective buying, intelligent coordination, programmable money." |

Optional: as a new account, paste *"I need 800 cartons of packaging materials delivered to Williamsburg within two weeks. I want to pay no more than $2.05 per carton."* into Create Demand.
